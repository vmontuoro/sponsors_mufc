How a pack wins

Each answer adds points to the packs listed. Highest total wins; second place becomes the runner-up. A tie breaks toward the pack sitting in the commitment band the sponsor chose.

Three things worth knowing
  • Program partner wins most often (62 of 256). Choosing “one program” in the last question adds 5 points on its own, which is enough to overturn most of what came before.
  • Grounds signage wins least often (16 of 256). It is rarely anyone’s top answer, but it is a frequent runner-up, so it still gets in front of sponsors.
  • 110 of the 256 paths come down to a point or less. 72 are won by exactly one point and 38 are dead heats settled by the commitment band in Q3. The runner-up shown underneath is a genuine second choice, not an afterthought.
